Our Heritage & Story

Where Tradition Meets Contemporary Passion

Founded in the historic dining district, Savoria was envisioned as a sanctuary for heartfelt gastronomic exploration. Under the guidance of Executive Chef Lorenzo Moretti, our kitchen honors ancestral Italian slow-cooking while embracing avant-garde European gastronomy.

We cultivate deep relationships with regional farmers and line-caught fisheries, ensuring every dish reflects seasonal purity, ethical sustainability, and peerless flavor depth.
